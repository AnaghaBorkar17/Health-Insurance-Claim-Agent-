SECTION 1: INPATIENT HOSPITALIZATION & ACCOMMODATION
================================================================================

Clause 1.1 - Room Rent and Boarding Expenses
In the event of inpatient admission exceeding 24 hours, room rent, boarding, and nursing charges shall be covered up to a maximum daily sub-limit of 1% of the total Sum Insured. Intensive Care Unit (ICU) charges are capped at 2% of Sum Insured per day. Pro-rata proportionate deductions will apply to all associated hospital charges if an insured patient chooses a room category exceeding this entitlement.

Clause 1.2 - Daycare Treatment Procedures
Medically necessary treatments and minor surgical procedures undertaken under general or local anesthesia in a hospital or registered daycare center that require less than 24 hours hospitalization due to technological advancement are covered, subject to pre-authorization.

================================================================================
SECTION 2: SPECIFIC PROCEDURES & DISEASE-SPECIFIC SUB-LIMITS
================================================================================

Clause 2.1 - Cataract Treatment Sub-Limit
Coverage for cataract surgery (phacoemulsification with intraocular lens implantation) is subject to an express sub-limit of $40,000 (or INR 40,000) per eye. Any surgical expenditure, consumable cost, or premium multifocal lens charge exceeding this sub-limit must be borne directly by the insured patient as an out-of-pocket copay.

Clause 2.2 - Joint Replacement and Orthopedic Implants
Total knee replacement (TKR) or total hip replacement (THR) for degenerative arthritis is payable up to $250,000 per joint, subject to a continuous waiting period of 24 months from the initial policy commencement date.

Clause 2.3 - Emergency Appendectomy and Hernia Repair
Acute emergency surgical treatment for appendicitis (ICD-10 K35.80, CPT 44970) or incarcerated hernia is covered in full without waiting period restrictions, provided the procedure is certified as emergent by the attending surgeon.

================================================================================
SECTION 3: WAITING PERIODS & PRE-EXISTING CONDITIONS
================================================================================

Clause 3.1 - Initial 30-Day Waiting Period
No claim shall be payable during the initial 30 days from policy commencement date, except for hospitalization necessitated directly by an acute accidental injury occurring after policy issuance.

Clause 3.2 - Pre-Existing Diseases (PED) Waiting Period
Any medical condition, ailment, or injury that was diagnosed, treated, or for which medical advice was sought within 36 months prior to the initial policy issuance shall be considered a Pre-Existing Disease. PED-related claims will be covered only after 24 months of continuous, uninterrupted policy renewal.

================================================================================
SECTION 4: PERMANENT EXCLUSIONS
================================================================================

Clause 4.1 - Cosmetic and Aesthetic Procedures
Any surgery, treatment, or intervention performed solely for aesthetic, cosmetic, or plastic appearance enhancement—including but not limited to rhinoplasty (nose reshaping), cosmetic blepharoplasty, liposuction, breast augmentation/reduction, and hair transplant—is strictly excluded from policy coverage. Exception: Reconstructive surgery required solely to restore functional impairment directly caused by accidental trauma or burn injury is admissible.

Clause 4.2 - Experimental and Unproven Treatments
Expenses related to unproven, experimental, or unscientific therapies, holistic treatments without published clinical trial efficacy, or medical devices not approved by national medical regulatory authorities are entirely excluded.

Clause 4.3 - Non-Medical Items and Personal Comfort Amenities
Personal comfort expenses, telephone charges, visitor meals, luxury suite surcharges, and non-prescribed cosmetic toiletries are non-payable items.

================================================================================
SECTION 5: MANDATORY CLAIM SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

Clause 5.1 - Pre-Authorization Documentation Checklist
Every pre-authorization claim request submitted by the treating hospital or the insured must include all of the following mandatory documents:
1. Completed Pre-Authorization Claim Form duly signed and stamped by the attending physician and hospital authority.
2. Itemized Cost Estimate and Pro-forma Billing Breakdown detailing room, surgeon fees, medicines, and consumables.
3. Relevant Diagnostic / Pathology / Radiology Investigation Reports supporting the primary diagnosis.
4. Physician Admission Clinical Notes / Past Medical History indicating whether the condition is acute or pre-existing.
Failure to submit any of these mandatory records shall prompt a formal Request for Information (Query), pausing claim adjudication until complete records are received.
